# IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) — Global Overview

## What is IEEE RAS?
The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) is a professional society within
IEEE (the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers). It is one of the
largest and most active technical societies in IEEE, dedicated to the science
and engineering of robotics and automation.

## Mission and Vision
Mission: To foster the development and facilitate the exchange of scientific
and technological knowledge in robotics and automation that benefits members,
the profession, and humanity.

Vision: To be the most recognized and respected global organization for
robotics and automation, uniting researchers, engineers, and students across
more than 100 countries.

## Fields of Interest
- Robotics research: manipulation, locomotion, perception, and control systems
- Industrial and manufacturing automation
- Human-robot interaction
- Aerial, marine, and field robotics
- Surgical and medical robotics
- Autonomous systems and AI in robotics

## Technical Committees
IEEE RAS maintains more than 30 specialized Technical Committees (TCs)
covering areas such as AI in robotics, aerial robotics, marine robotics,
surgical robotics, and human-robot interaction, connecting global experts in
each sub-field.

## Flagship Conferences and Publications
- ICRA — IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation
- IROS — IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems
- CASE — IEEE International Conference on Automation Science and Engineering
- IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) — a flagship journal
These venues receive over 10,000 submissions annually and are considered
among the most prestigious in the robotics research community.

## Student Activities
IEEE RAS supports the next generation of roboticists through:
- Student travel grants to attend conferences
- Robotics competitions (e.g., teams competing in ICRA-affiliated challenges)
- Technical education programs and workshops
- A global network of IEEE RAS Student Branch Chapters at universities

## What is an IEEE RAS Student Branch Chapter?
A Student Branch Chapter (SBC) is a local chapter formed under an existing
IEEE Student Branch at a university. It gives students direct access to the
RAS professional community and typically:
- Organizes workshops, guest lectures, and technical sessions on robotics and
  automation
- Runs hands-on project teams and hosts internal competitions
- Connects student members to the wider IEEE RAS network, funding
  opportunities, and mentorship
- Helps members develop both technical skills (robotics, embedded systems,
  AI/ML for robotics) and soft skills (leadership, presentation, teamwork)
