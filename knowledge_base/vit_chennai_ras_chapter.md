# IEEE RAS Student Branch Chapter — VIT Chennai

## Identity
- Full name: Vellore Institute of Technology, Chennai — IEEE Robotics and
  Automation Society (RAS) Student Branch Chapter.
- The chapter operates under the IEEE Student Branch at VIT Chennai, within
  IEEE Region 10 (Asia and Pacific), India.
- VIT Chennai's IEEE presence also includes other technical society chapters
  (e.g., Power and Energy Society, Vehicular Technology Society), of which
  RAS is one.

## Founding / Inauguration
- The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) Student Branch Chapter at
  VIT Chennai was formally inaugurated on 9 October 2024 at the Sir M V
  Auditorium, held jointly with the IEEE Day 2024 celebrations.
- The inauguration event featured chief guests from the IEEE RAS Bangalore
  Section, including its Chair and Secretary, along with a guest of honor
  from academia, reflecting close ties between the student chapter and the
  broader RAS professional community in the region.
- The event's agenda included a welcome address, lamp-lighting ceremony, and
  keynote talks on emerging technologies in robotics and automation, aimed at
  fostering innovation and collaboration among the IEEE student community.

## Purpose at VIT Chennai
Like other IEEE RAS student chapters, the VIT Chennai chapter exists to:
- Give students hands-on exposure to robotics and automation beyond the
  classroom curriculum
- Organize technical workshops, guest lectures, and project-based learning
  in robotics, embedded systems, and automation
- Provide a platform to network with IEEE RAS professionals, alumni, and
  peers from other student chapters
- Encourage participation in IEEE RAS-affiliated student competitions and
  funding/travel-grant opportunities
