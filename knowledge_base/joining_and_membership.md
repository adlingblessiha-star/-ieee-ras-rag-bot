# Joining IEEE RAS and Membership Benefits

## How students typically join
1. Become an IEEE Student Member (via ieee.org), which gives access to the
   parent IEEE organization.
2. Add the Robotics and Automation Society as a society membership, which
   unlocks RAS-specific benefits and access to the local Student Branch
   Chapter's activities.
3. Register for and attend chapter events (workshops, info sessions,
   inauguration/kickoff meetings) advertised through the IEEE Student Branch
   or the chapter's social media.
4. Many chapters also welcome non-members to attend open workshops as a way
   to explore before formally joining.

## Typical membership benefits
- Access to IEEE Xplore Digital Library and IEEE Press eBooks
- Discounted or free access to select development software and tools
- Eligibility for student travel grants and chapter initiative grants for
  robotics projects and events
- Opportunities to compete in IEEE RAS-affiliated robotics competitions
- Networking with IEEE RAS professionals, faculty advisors, and industry
  experts through chapter-hosted talks and workshops
- Leadership opportunities within the student chapter (e.g., chair,
  secretary, treasurer, technical/media roles)

## Getting involved beyond membership
Most chapters, including student RAS chapters, run project or activity teams
where members build robotics/automation projects hands-on, in addition to
attending talks and workshops. Reach out to the chapter's current leadership
(via their Instagram/LinkedIn or IEEE Student Branch contact) to ask about
open project teams or upcoming events.
